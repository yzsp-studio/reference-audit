## 🇨🇳 中文

用户提交论文、学位论文或参考文献清单后，系统审查参考文献真实性、题录准确性、文内—文后对应关系，以及正文主张是否得到被引文献支持。产出专业、清晰、可直接指导修改的论文引用审计报告与飞书文档。审计遵循三条原则：全量看见、风险聚焦；先辨角色、再查支持；证据深度服从问题。开放主题综述、普通论文精读、论文代写或仅做语言润色时不使用。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 提交论文/参考文献清单 → 输出可直接照改的引用审计报告
- 查真实性：识别不存在或无法核实的参考文献
- 查题录准确性：作者、年份、出处、页码逐项核对
- 查文内—文后对应关系，揪出错引与漏引
- 判断正文主张是否真被被引文献支持，识别过度推断与二手转引
- 按「全量看见、风险聚焦」出报告，并交付飞书文档

## 🇬🇧 English

After you submit a paper, thesis or reference list, it audits whether the references actually exist, whether the bibliographic entries are accurate, whether in-text citations match the reference list, and whether the paper's claims are genuinely supported by the works cited. It produces a professional, clear citation-audit report that can directly guide revision, plus a Feishu doc. The audit follows three principles: see everything but focus on risk, identify each source's role before checking support, and let evidence depth follow the question. It is not for open-topic reviews, ordinary close reading of papers, ghostwriting or language polishing alone.

### ✨ What it does

- Paper or reference list in, a revision-ready citation audit report out
- Authenticity check flags references that cannot be verified
- Entry accuracy check verifies authors, years, venues and pages
- Matches in-text citations against the reference list to find wrong or missing ones
- Tests whether claims are truly supported, spotting overreach and secondhand citation
- Applies see-everything, focus-on-risk auditing and delivers a Feishu doc
